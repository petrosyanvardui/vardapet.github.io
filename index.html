VARDAPET

Director · Digital Creator · Cultural Researcher

VARDAPET is the independent artistic identity of Vardui Petrosyan, spanning film direction, visual storytelling and cultural research.

This multidisciplinary, author-driven creative practice is rooted in Armenian heritage, shaped by a cross-cultural journey from her Siberian homeland through Europe and the United States, and currently based in Germany.

It explores identity, faith, memory, freedom and the human condition.
